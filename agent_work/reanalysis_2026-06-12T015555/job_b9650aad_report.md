The JD makes clear that the job is embedded in the “Performance & Risk Solutions Engineering (‘PreSales’) team,” and that its purpose is to support “sales teams navigating complex risk use cases” while also “bringing feedback to the product team” [JD]. That combination suggests a classic pre-sales boundary role: it translates technical product capability into client value, handles objections, and helps convert sophisticated prospect needs into a compelling solution narrative [JD].

The business problem is not generic software selling; it is selling quantitative investment-risk capabilities to institutional clients who care about model credibility and workflow fit. The role focuses on “multi-asset risk,” “ex-ante risk/tracking error,” “stress testing,” “single security analytics,” and “fixed income products and derivatives” [JD]. Those signals indicate the clients are not buying a simple dashboard — they are evaluating a risk framework, its assumptions, and whether it is trustworthy for investment decision-making [JD]. Research reinforces that this is a client-facing pre-sales function serving buy-side firms such as hedge funds, asset owners, and asset managers, and that the role is about demos, discovery, RFPs, and proof-of-concept motions rather than implementation engineering [RESEARCH].

Likely organizational placement: Americas Solution Engineering within the risk/performance product go-to-market motion, interfacing with Sales, Product, and prospects/customers [JD]. The JD also names “Axioma risk analytics tool” [JD], which suggests the role is tied to SimCorp’s risk analytics product line and requires enough domain depth to credibly represent the platform in front of quant-oriented buyers [INFERENCE]. Research clarifies that this sits on the pre-sales side, not the product-building side [RESEARCH].
