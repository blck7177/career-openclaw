## Company Overview
Clearwater Analytics (NYSE: CWAN) is a SaaS-based fintech founded in 2004 and headquartered in Boise, Idaho, with offices in New York, London, Edinburgh, and Noida. The company provides a cloud-native investment management platform for institutional investors: insurers, asset managers, hedge funds, banks, corporations, and governments. The platform aggregates and normalizes data on over $10 trillion in global invested assets daily. Revenue model is subscription SaaS.

The core platform integrates: investment accounting and reporting, performance measurement, compliance monitoring, planning and order management, and risk analytics — all on a single-instance, multi-tenant architecture.
