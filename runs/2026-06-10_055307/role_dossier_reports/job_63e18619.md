- Updating governance documentation, process steps, and reporting packs to reflect current risk posture. [INFERENCE]
- Preparing management reports showing the overall ICT risk profile and progress of mitigation efforts. [JD]

Tools and systems are not central to the role, but the JD suggests likely interaction with **ServiceNow, Jira, Confluence, and G Suite**, which probably support case tracking, documentation, and coordination. [JD] The role may also interact with technical topics such as database security, web application firewalls, Linux environments, and possibly AI/LLM-related risk considerations, but the JD does not show that this is a deep engineering role; these are more likely contextual risk domains the manager must understand enough to evaluate and challenge. Success in the role probably looks like: ICT risks are handled through a standardized process, mitigation actions are visible and moving, leadership has a clear picture of the risk posture, and the organization can demonstrate compliance and control maturity to internal or external stakeholders.
